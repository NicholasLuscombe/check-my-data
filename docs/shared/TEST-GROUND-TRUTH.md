## Conventions

- Channel attributions in this file are engine records. Per-test p-values and channel credits were measured from tool output at the sessions named against them. They cannot referee a change to the engine's calibration, because they record what the engine did rather than what is in the data. A fixture's construction is the independent anchor; its recorded channel p-values are not. **S340 sharpening:** they are engine records *at one draw*. A record written from a single seed cannot distinguish a stable channel from a coin-flip.
- **Adjudicate inside the planted region, not against the file.** A test can carry a fixture's severity while firing outside the mechanism the fixture was built to plant — DS22's retired Runs channel returned p ≈ 0.98 inside its planted window, and DS12b's Regional Noise returns p ≈ 0.81 inside its own. The method is a per-slice measurement: run the test inside the planted rows and columns and outside them, separately, and compare. Precedent at `batch-fixtures.mjs:88` (DS10). **Every channel attribution in this file predating S341 was made without one, apart from DS10's.** Of the two entries examined by the method at S341, DS08's channel list was incomplete and its mechanism misattributed, and DS12b's declared band was never derived from the construction at all. The remaining entries have not been re-examined and their attributions carry that qualification. **S358 held the rule a third time**, on DS12b again and from the construction rather than from tool output: measured per half, neither half of the file flags alone and the firing sits in the honest one. **S359 held it a fourth time, and widened the instrument:** one file against one file cannot separate a fabrication response from a property of the statistic, so the adjudication ran over all 27 fixtures. **Related corpus fact:** DS03, DS04, DS09 and DS10 interleave their condition label one row at a time, so every row is a transition and any changepoint-to-boundary distance is zero by construction. Any measurement on the row-block axis has six fixtures to work with, not ten.
- **A firing on a clean fixture is never declared** (S340). It is a calibration defect to fix, not a channel to record.
- **A channel is declared only if it holds across the full seed sweep** (S340). One-seed evidence supports "indeterminate", not "declared".
- **Do not read the absence of a HIGH as evidence about effect size.** The census of which tests can reach HIGH has been wrong twice and is size-dependent. As measured at S341 across all 29 tests: Cross-Condition Rank Correlation carries an unconditional `HIGH → MODERATE` cap and can never reach it at any p; Modality Test is blocked by both a hardcoded `P_FLOOR` clamp and a dip gate, on a test whose bootstrap was retired at S159b; Entropy / Zipf is blocked by the `2/(1+B)` doubling floor; Residual Spike Correlation by a `1/(B+1)` floor at fixed B = 999; Constant-Offset Blocks by an observed floor of exactly 1.000e-3 at its highest declared count, which `flagFromP` reads as MODERATE because HIGH needs strictly less; and Windowed Autocorrelation and Cross-Condition Consistency by grid coarseness at every count they take. **Column Goodness-of-Fit is reachable** — the S339 census was wrong about it; BH step-up with monotinicity enforcement returns the raw `2/2001` floor when the whole tested family sits flat there. The count rises to ten if every fixture sat on the coarse side of the row-count branch, and is ten at the 199 tier, which no fixture has ever run.
- **Resample counts branch on row count, and the branch was never tested against results.** Eight tests take a smaller count on larger inputs. Every branch cites wallclock; none cites a threshold. Measured at S341: forcing all eight to their highest declared count costs +8% on the battery and moves three tiers upward at 8/8 seeds, one of which moves a declared band. The saving the branch defends is 3.4 seconds. Two tests are coarse-by-default across most of the corpus (LOESS, Regional Noise); Blocked Mahalanobis has never run its coarse branch on any fixture in the suite.
- **Resample counts are independently adjustable** (S340, supersedes the S339 convention recorded here). Per-test PRNG streams landed — `createPRNGFactory(matrix)` → `rngFor(testId)`, keyed on the dispatch-map key at both call sites. Coupling inside a test is preserved and owned by that test; coupling between tests is gone. The earlier statement that changing any count displaces every downstream test's p-values is retired. **A dispatch-key rename is now a statistical change.**
- **Severity scale:** 0 = CLEAN, 1 = MINOR, 2 = ELEVATED, 3 = CRITICAL. Engine output is compared to GT by strict equality.
- **Batch test:** `node test/validate-batch.mjs` runs 27 fixtures and prints pass/fail; the runner reports out of 28 because the DS01 cross-shape invariance check is appended after the loop. Any new-below-GT delta on a fabricated dataset is a regression requiring investigation before commit.
- **Pending-verification lane** (S108): fabricated fixtures whose target test is not yet implemented in the active build are marked `pending: true` in the GT lookup and reported as `◦ pending` rather than pass/fail. DS22 is the current occupant pending Track E (a) Blocked Mahalanobis (S109 target).
- **Attribution-pending flag** (S108 convention, narrative): fixtures where the current severity GT match is driven by collateral channels rather than the intended forensic target are noted in the table with explicit language ("severity driven by X FP, intended Y"); GT severity stands until v1.1 methodology audit resolves the attribution, at which point the entry is re-verified. DS21 was the occupant pending parked #15 + #18. S109 close-out: DS21 GT revised 3 → 2 (see §Accepted deltas); attribution-pending flag lifted. **Current occupant (S341): DS12b**, whose severity 1 rests entirely on a boundary artefact and a false positive while its intended target measures the wrong sign. **S358:** the false-positive half is now adjudicated from the construction and reproduced independently; the boundary-artefact half is unresolved and waits on DS12a's comparator reading. **S359: DS12b vacates.** Its severity is now attributed to a channel that fires at the plant boundary, measured by census. The occupant slot is empty; the intended-target failure on that fixture is a separate item (P53).
- **Fixture additions:** generate-test-datasets.py carries a fixed seed (7741 for legacy fixtures; per-fixture locked seeds for post-S98 additions — DS19 = 20260420, DS20 = 20260421, DS21 = 20260422, DS22 = 20260423). New fixtures append with a new DS-id, one new row in the table above, and a session summary documenting the construction and rationale.
- **Fixture revisions:** severity changes OR construction pivots (v1 → v2) require a corresponding GT update here, recorded in the §Accepted deltas section with session reference.
- **These fixtures have been in continuous use since roughly S10 and every threshold, gate and count in the battery was chosen while watching what they do.** That is fitting to the test set. Whatever the battery's true false-positive and false-negative rates are, the numbers this corpus reports are optimistic by an unknown margin. The corpus is a regression instrument and should be read as one; it cannot measure the calibration of a tool that was shaped around it. Independent measurement needs a null harness that generates fresh clean data at run time, and a held-out dose-response set for the false-negative side.
